With $300B+ under administration and 700,000+ LPs on platform, we’ve built the scale to match our ambition. And with JunieAI, our purpose-built AI platform, we’re reimagining how private markets operate, embedding intelligence across every workflow. Founder-led since 2014, backed by $350M+ in funding, and now 1,000+ employees strong, we’re building a company designed to shape the future of private markets for decades to come.

Our culture is built for people who want to do ambitious, meaningful work alongside exceptionally talented teammates. We think like owners, move with urgency, and take pride in solving hard problems that truly matter to our customers and the future of private markets. We believe the best ideas come from open debate, deep collaboration, and diverse perspectives, which is why we believe transparency is the default and feedback makes us stronger. If you’re energized by high standards, rapid growth, and the opportunity to help define a category at a pivotal moment, come join us!

Juniper Square offers employees a variety of ways to work, ranging from a fully remote experience to working full-time in one of our physical offices. We invest heavily in digital-first (https://blog.junipersquare.com/juniper-square-ponders-future-of-office-with-digital-first-hybrid-workplace-strategy/) operations, allowing our teams to collaborate effectively across 27 U.S. states, 2 Canadian Provinces, India, Luxembourg, and England. ## About the AI Guild

The AI Guild leads internal AI adoption at Juniper Square. We partner with teams across the company to identify high-impact opportunities, build the applications that solve them, and drive the adoption that turns AI capability into business outcomes.

## About your role

We are hiring Forward Deployed AI Engineers to embed with business teams and lead the end-to-end delivery of internal applications, from discovery through production adoption. You will partner directly with stakeholders to scope the work, design the solution, build it, and see it through to active use.

This is an AI-first engineering role. The applications you build will be powered by modern AI, and you will use agentic coding tools as your primary development environment.

The role combines product management and full-stack engineering, and calls for someone who operates with high agency. Working from broad strategic priorities set by Juniper Square leadership, you will conduct discovery with stakeholders, define product requirements, sequence delivery, and make the trade-offs between scope, speed, and quality that get working software into users' hands quickly.

Success is measured by adoption and impact. You will own the products you build through launch and beyond, iterating based on real user feedback and contributing the patterns you develop back to the broader AI Guild.

## What you'll do

- Partner with business teams (Operations, Customer Success, Marketing, Finance, Legal, and others) to identify opportunities where AI can improve internal workflows and outcomes
- Conduct discovery with stakeholders and translate business needs into clear product requirements and acceptance criteria
- Carry each product through its full lifecycle, from prototyping and stakeholder validation through hardening, deployment, and rollout to production
- Use agentic coding tools such as Claude Code, Cursor, or Augment as the primary development environment
- Operate independently on standalone internal applications, and partner with central Engineering teams when work intersects with core systems or production platforms
- Make engineering tradeoffs appropriate to an internal user base, balancing speed of delivery with reliability, security, and maintainability
- Own deployed tools through adoption, including post-launch support, iteration based on user feedback, and ongoing maintenance
- Produce documentation, conduct user training, and manage rollouts to drive adoption
- Contribute reusable components, patterns, and learnings back to AI teams

## Qualifications

- 4+ years of professional experience shipping production software end to end, with hands-on experience building AI-enabled applications
- Demonstrated ability to translate ambiguous business needs into product requirements and deliver working software against them
- Full-stack engineering capability across data, services, and user interfaces
- Proficiency with agentic coding tools such as Claude Code, Cursor, or Augment
- Experience deploying and operating applications on a major cloud provider
- Working knowledge of application security practices for systems handling sensitive company data
- Strong product judgment, including the ability to define scope for an initial release, prioritize features, and determine completion criteria
- Excellent written communication skills across requirements, specifications, documentation, and stakeholder updates
- Proven ability to work directly with non-technical stakeholders and communicate technical concepts clearly
