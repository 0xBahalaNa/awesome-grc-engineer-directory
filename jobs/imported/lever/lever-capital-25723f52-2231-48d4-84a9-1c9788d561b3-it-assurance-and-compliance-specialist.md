We are a leading trading platform that is ambitiously expanding to the four corners of the globe. Our top-rated products have won prestigious industry awards for their cutting-edge technology and seamless client experience. We deliver only the best, so we are always in search of the best people to join our ever-growing talented team.

As part of our ongoing global expansion, we are seeking an IT Assurance and Compliance Specialist to join our team. This is a newly created position with a global scope, offering the opportunity to shape and influence our compliance framework worldwide.

### Responsibilities:

- Support the preparation of internal and external IT audits, regulatory requests, and other compliance questionnaires — assist with evidence gathering, coordinate with technical teams and other stakeholders (e.g., Legal, Compliance, Finance) on assigned items, and help compile complete audit packages;

- Contribute to gap assessments against regulatory requirements — document findings and help draft remediation plans with owners and deadlines, under guidance from senior team members;

- Support reviews of IT policies, standards, and procedures — providing input to help ensure alignment with regulatory requirements;

- Support control documentation and monitoring within the GRC platform (e.g., Vanta);

- Support ongoing certification maintenance (e.g., PCI DSS, ISO 27001) by helping coordinate required actions and evidence.

### Requirements:

- 2+ years in IT compliance, IT audit, or IT governance;

- Hands-on experience with gap assessments and supporting remediation plans;

- Knowledge of at least one of: DORA, PCI DSS, SOC 2, NIST, or similar frameworks;

- Comfortable handling day-to-day compliance tasks independently, while knowing when to involve a senior colleague on complex or ambiguous issues;

- Good communication skills — able to explain regulatory requirements in practical terms to technical teams;

- Strong attention to detail;

- Experience in FinTech, banking, or another regulated industry is a strong plus;

- Experience working with a GRC platform (e.g., Vanta) is a strong plus.
