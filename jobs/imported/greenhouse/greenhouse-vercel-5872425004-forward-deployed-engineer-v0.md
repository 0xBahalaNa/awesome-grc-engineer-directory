<div class="content-intro"><h2>About Vercel:</h2>
<p><span data-sheets-root="1">Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company. We free people and agents to ship what’s next.</span></p>
<p><span data-sheets-root="1">For more than a decade, Vercel has shaped how the web is built. As the team behind Next.js, v0, and AI SDK, we create products that help builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience.</span></p>
<p><span data-sheets-root="1">Now, software is entering a new era, and the next generation of products will not just be used by people. They will be built, extended, and operated by agents.</span></p>
<p><span data-sheets-root="1">We are building the platform for that future, trusted by companies like&nbsp;<strong>OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide</strong>. Whether you’re building our products, supporting our customers, growing our community, or shaping our story, you’ll help define what comes next.</span></p></div><h2><strong>What You Will Do</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li>Own end-to-end implementations with enterprise customers, from kickoff through production, working through whatever obstacles come up along the way.</li>
<li>Understand customer pain points deeply and translate them into concrete product solutions, feeding signal back to engineering and product teams.</li>
<li>Build internal tools and processes that drive efficiency and scalability across the FDE function.</li>
<li>Collaborate with cross-functional teams including GTM, product, and core engineering to deliver large-scale projects with cross-team dependencies.</li>
<li>Run on-site workshops, discovery sessions, and technical enablement with enterprise customers.</li>
<li>Maintain and operate solutions at high scale, ensuring the highest levels of security and reliability for customer deployments.</li>
<li>Implement identity synchronization and lifecycle management solutions using SCIM, SSO, SAML, and other enterprise standards.</li>
<li>Work with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, AWS CloudFormation, Kubernetes) to deliver scalable, high-availability applications.</li>
<li>Build and maintain high-quality software using TypeScript, Next.js, React, and REST APIs, including direct contributions to the v0 codebase.</li>
<li>Be a daily practitioner of AI-assisted development: v0, Cursor, Claude, Codex, and whatever comes next.</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>About You</strong></h2>
<p><strong>Core qualifications:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Power user of AI coding tools. You use v0, Cursor, Claude, Codex, or equivalent daily and have a current, working understanding of the AI coding landscape: agents, MCP, context management, local vs. remote execution.</li>
<li>Proficient in Next.js, React, and TypeScript. You're comfortable working directly in a production codebase.</li>
<li>Experience in a GTM-adjacent engineering role, whether as a Forward Deployed Engineer, Sales Engineer, Solutions Engineer, or similar. You know how to navigate a sales cycle without losing your technical integrity.</li>
<li>Experience working directly with enterprise customers: running discovery, building trust, re-engaging at-risk accounts, and finding internal champions.</li>
<li>Willingness to travel to customer sites regularly.</li>
<li>Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.</li>
<li>Comfortable working autonomously and making decisions with minimal supervision in a fast-moving environment.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Bonus points:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Experience with enterprise integration work:</li>
<ul>
<li><em>Accounts:</em> SSO, account provisioning, RBAC configuration and implementation.</li>
<li><em>Relationship building:</em> cultivating internal champions, running on-site workshops, re-engaging at-risk customers.</li>
<li><em>Security and Legal:</em> translating security requirements, navigating procurement and legal review processes.</li>
</ul>
<li>Data chops. Comfortable writing SQL to understand customer health, usage trends, and spot expansion opportunities.</li>
<li>Familiarity with access control models like RBAC, ABAC, and ReBAC.</li>
<li>Background in cloud infrastructure and distributed systems (AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform).</li>
<li>Strong knowledge of security architecture: LDAP, Active Directory, SAML, OAuth2, OpenID Connect.</li>
</ul>
