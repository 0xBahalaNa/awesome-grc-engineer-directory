&lt;p style="line-height: 1;"&gt;&lt;em&gt;&lt;span data-teams="true"&gt;*This is a fully remote position and is only available for people located in LATAM*&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/em&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Insight Assurance is a global audit firm on a mission to transform how organizations achieve cybersecurity and compliance. Founded by former Big 4 (EY) professionals, we deliver next-generation audit services across SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS (QSA), HITRUST, CMMC (C3PAO), and FedRAMP (3PAO) frameworks.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We’re not your traditional audit firm — we’re tech-enabled, leveraging compliance automation and advanced collaboration tools to make audits faster, smarter, and more impactful for our clients.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Recognized on the Inc. 5000 and Fast 50 lists, Insight Assurance is one of the fastest-growing global audit firms, with 170+ professionals supporting nearly 2,000 clients across the Americas, EMEA, and APAC.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p style="line-height: 1;"&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Role Summary&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As a Senior IT/GRC Auditor, you’ll lead day-to-day activities for SOC 2 and other IT compliance engagements. You will guide staff, engage with clients, and play a key role in the delivery of high-quality audits and readiness assessments.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p style="line-height: 1;"&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Key Responsibilities&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;- Lead ITGC and application control testing efforts&lt;br&gt;- Guide and review work completed by junior team members&lt;br&gt;- Perform walkthroughs, testing, and documentation for SOC 2, SOC 1, and HIPAA engagements&lt;br&gt;- Identify control gaps and provide recommendations for remediation&lt;br&gt;- Maintain strong client relationships through clear and proactive communication&lt;br&gt;- Ensure timely progress updates and escalate issues to management&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p style="line-height: 1;"&gt;&nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p style="line-height: 1;"&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Requirements&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;- 3–4 years of experience performing IT audits, with a focus on ITGC and application controls&lt;br&gt;- Strong working knowledge of SOC 2 and related frameworks&lt;br&gt;- Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, MIS, Cybersecurity, or a related field&lt;br&gt;- Strong client communication and project management skills&lt;br&gt;- Bilingual in English required&lt;br&gt;&lt;br&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Nice to Have&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;br&gt;&lt;br&gt;- Experience with GRC tools (e.g., Vanta, Drata, Secureframe)&lt;br&gt;- Familiarity with cloud environments such as AWS, Azure, or GCP&lt;br&gt;- Progress toward CISA, CPA, CISSP, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor&lt;/p&gt;
